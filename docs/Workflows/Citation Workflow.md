# Citation workflow

Use citations to support attribution and contact hypotheses after structural
graph targets have been established.

## 1. Pin inputs

Use the exact normalized paper document and corpus artifact identities. Prefer
DOI, arXiv, and canonical URL identities for bibliography resolution.

## 2. Resolve conservatively

Resolve exact identifiers first. Retain ambiguous and unresolved entries rather
than forcing a target. Keep marker locations and bibliography offsets.

## 3. Bind local context

When a citation accompanies a graph annotation, bind it to that exact passage
and graph target. Record whether the authors claim adoption, extension,
comparison, background, or another relationship.

A citation outside a validated graph annotation remains document-level
awareness or provenance. A technique phrase is not itself a graph target.

## 4. Review

Confirm the document digest, exact excerpt, graph digest, target ID,
bibliography resolution, and asserted relationship. Preserve rejected and
conflicting bindings.

## 5. Infer separately

Structural distance remains graph-only. Citation evidence may help explain a
shared region as contact rather than convergence, but it does not change the
region's signature or edit cost.
