# Phylodigy

A **phylodigital tree** is a tree-like lineage hypothesis for digital
artifacts. It derives from observable structure and evidence.

**Phylodigital** is the adjective. **Phylodigy** is the project and the noun
for this line of analysis.

Phylodigy represents each executable ML artifact as an evidence-backed graph
profile of operators and layers.

It compares graph-derived character vectors, diagnoses tree-likeness, infers a
phylogeny, and can retain explicit dated contact evidence separately.

Framework graphs provide structural evidence. Papers name and describe exact
graph objects; they do not define or infer the structures themselves.

Structural distances and tree placement use graph evidence only. Dates,
citations, source history, and paper annotations remain explicit external
evidence for interpretation and contact analysis.

> [!warning] Interpretation limit
> The lineage and contact artifacts contain reproducible hypotheses. They do not prove historical causation or biological ancestry.
